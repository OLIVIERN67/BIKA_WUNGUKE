# BIKA WUNGUKE — User Guide

**Bika neza. Cunga neza. Wunguke kurushaho.**

This guide explains, in plain language, how to use BIKA WUNGUKE to record your business money.
It is written for small traders, so it avoids technical words.

---

## 1. What is BIKA WUNGUKE?

BIKA WUNGUKE is a simple tool that helps you:

- write down the money that **comes in** (income / sales),
- write down the money that **goes out** (expenses / costs),
- keep track of the money you **save**,
- and see a clear **summary** of how your business is doing.

You can use it on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

---

## 2. Creating your account

1. Open the BIKA WUNGUKE website.
2. Click **Get started**.
3. Fill in your **full name** and **phone number**. The phone number is how you will sign in.
4. Choose a **password** with at least 6 characters. Keep it secret.
5. (Optional) Add your **business details**:
   - **Business name** — for example *Uwase Shop*.
   - **Business category** — for example *Retail and trade* or *Farming*.
   - **Business location** — for example *Kigali, Nyabugogo*.
   - **Business description** — a short sentence about what you sell or do.
   - **Preferred language** — Kinyarwanda (default) or English.
   - **Currency** — RWF by default.
6. Click **Create account**.

You are now signed in and ready to record your business.

> Tip: Write your password somewhere safe. If you forget it, you will not be able to sign in.

---

## 3. Signing in

1. Click **Sign in**.
2. Enter your **phone number** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

---

## 4. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen after you sign in. It shows:

- **Total income** — all the money you have recorded coming in.
- **Total expenses** — all the money you have recorded going out.
- **Net cash flow** — income minus expenses. If it is positive, you made money.
- **Savings balance** — deposits minus withdrawals, shown separately from expenses.
- **Cash flow chart** — a picture of your income and expenses over time.
- **Expenses by category** — where your money went.
- **Savings goals** — progress towards each savings goal you have set.
- **Recent transactions** — your five latest records.

Use the **Period** buttons to change the time window:

- **This week** — from Monday of this week until today.
- **This month** — from the first day of this month until today.
- **Custom range** — choose your own **From** and **To** dates.

> Important: **Net cash flow is calculated only from the records you enter** in BIKA WUNGUKE.
> It equals your recorded income minus your recorded expenses for the selected period. Savings are
> kept separately and are not counted as expenses, so money is never counted twice.

Use the **Add record** button at the top to quickly add income or an expense. If you have no
records in the selected period, you will see a friendly message inviting you to add your first one.

---

## 5. Adding an income or expense record

1. Click **Add record**.
2. Choose the type: **Income** (money in) or **Expense** (money out).
3. Type the **amount**.
4. Choose a **category** (for example *Sales (Mauzo)* or *Transport*). A category is **required**.
5. Choose the **date** (today is filled in for you).
6. Choose the **payment method** (Cash, Mobile Money, Bank, Credit, Other).
7. (Optional) Write a **description**, for example "Sold 3 crates of tomatoes".
8. Click **Save record**.

---

## 6. Viewing, editing and deleting records

1. Go to **Transactions** in the menu.
2. Use the **filters** to find records:
   - search by words in the description,
   - filter by type (income/expense),
   - filter by category,
   - or choose a date range.
3. To **edit** a record, click the pencil icon ✏️.
4. To **delete** a record, click the bin icon 🗑️ and confirm.

---

## 7. Managing categories

Categories help you group your records.

1. Go to **Categories** in the menu.
2. Your income and expense categories are shown in two groups.
3. Click **New category** to add one: give it a **name**, choose its **type**, and pick a **colour**.
4. To edit or delete, use the pencil or bin icons.

> New accounts already come with common categories, so you can start right away.

---

## 8. Savings

1. Go to **Savings** in the menu.
2. Click **Add savings**.
3. Choose **Deposit** (put money in) or **Withdrawal** (take money out).
4. Type the **amount**, the **date**, and (optional) a **goal** and a **note**.
5. Click **Save record**.

The top of the page shows total deposits, total withdrawals and your savings balance.

---

## 9. Reports and sharing

1. Go to **Reports** in the menu.
2. Choose a **date range** and (optional) a **type**.
3. Review the totals and the list of records.
4. Click **CSV** or **PDF** to download a report you can keep or share.

---

## 10. My Financial Record

This page shows a complete **statement** of everything you have recorded, for the whole time you
have used BIKA WUNGUKE.

1. Go to **My Financial Record** in the menu.
2. At the top you see your **business profile** (name, category, location, currency and language).
3. Below that are your overall totals: **total income**, **total expenses**, **net cash flow** and
   **savings balance**.
4. **Overall position** combines your net cash flow and your savings balance.
5. Two tables show your **income by category** and your **expenses by category**.
6. Click **Export a report** to download this information as CSV or PDF.

---

## 11. Settings

Go to **Settings** in the menu to:

- change your **profile** details (name, email, business name, **business category**,
  **business location**, **business description**, **preferred language**, currency),
- change your **password**.

---

## 12. Tips for good records

- Record each sale and each cost **on the same day**.
- Be honest and complete — better records give better decisions.
- Give every record a short description so you remember it later.
- Check your Dashboard every few days to see how you are doing.
